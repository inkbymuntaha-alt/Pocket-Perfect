❓ How to play — beginner guide
1. Start with supplies

Your stall begins with 5 of each product. Check Your Supplies before serving customers. Tap +3 to buy more stock; each purchase costs money.

2. Find customers

Tap Find customer to invite shoppers. Their baskets show what they want. You can keep several customers waiting in your queue.

3. Serve a basket

Make sure you have enough of every requested item, then tap Serve basket. You earn the selling price and reputation for a fair sale.

4. Set fair prices

Tap Price beside a product to cycle through selling prices. Higher prices can earn more Market Coins, but prices that are too high can hurt reputation.

5. Reach today's target

Watch the progress bar below your stall. Earn enough sales to reach the daily target for a bigger closing bonus and extra reputation.

6. Close and reopen

When ready, tap Close the day. Then tap Open next day to start fresh with a new event and a higher sales target.

7. Grow your market

Use Market Coins and reputation to buy upgrades. Some upgrades need both. They attract more customers, improve baskets or expand your market.

8. Build loyal customers

Serve familiar shoppers when they return. Every third visit from the same customer gives a loyalty bonus. Complete the weekly challenge by serving 12 customers.

Helpful tips: Check stock before serving, keep prices reasonable, use each day's special event to earn extra money, and save your progress on this device. Your goal is to grow from one small stall into a thriving neighbourhood market.
