## Information handled by this version

This prototype does not ask players to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or location.

Game progress (such as the player's virtual Market Coins, reputation, day, inventory and upgrades) is saved locally on the device using app/browser storage. This version does not provide cloud synchronisation.

The game code currently contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, account system, payment processing or in-app purchases. It has no third-party runtime scripts or remote content dependencies in the game page.

## How to delete local game progress

Players can clear the app's storage through Android system app settings. Uninstalling the app also removes its locally stored data in normal Android operation. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently erase game progress.
